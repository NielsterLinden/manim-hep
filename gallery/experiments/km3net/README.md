# KM3NeT: contributions welcome

This folder is empty for now. Ideas that would fit:

- a detection unit and its optical modules (DOMs) in the deep sea;
- Cherenkov light from a muon track reaching the PMTs, with photon arrival times;
- track-like versus shower-like events;
- atmospheric versus cosmic neutrino flux.

**Prior art.** [dvnanima](https://github.com/NeutrinoHit/dvnanima) animates neutrino
interactions, Cherenkov light and air showers in Manim. Its author may be a good person
to talk to before you start.

There is no KM3NeT preset in `manim_hep` yet. Use the generic theme, and never add
collaboration logos. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and open a
"Port this" or "Animation request" issue to discuss an example first.
